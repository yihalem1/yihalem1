## Yihalem Yimolal Tiruneh

I am an AI Research Scientist at [ROBROS](https://www.robros.co.kr/), a humanoid robotics company in Seoul, where I work on world action models for robot manipulation.

I received my M.S. (2026) and B.S. (2024) in Computer Science and Engineering from [UNIST](https://www.unist.ac.kr/), where I worked with [Seungryul Baek](https://sites.google.com/site/bsrvision00) and [Binod Bhattarai](https://sites.google.com/view/bbinod/home) on 3D human reconstruction and hand–object interaction.

### Research

I am interested in robot foundation models that learn from human video, robot data, and simulation, and in how 3D structure and predictive world models help them generalize across tasks, environments, and embodiments.
